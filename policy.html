Booking

DM me with the player's name, number, and team. After the game I send proofs, you pick your photos, you pay, I deliver.

Payment is due when you pick your photos, before delivery.

I can't promise a set number of shots of any one person. If I didn't get enough for the package you wanted, you can drop to a smaller one or pass. You won't be charged for photos I couldn't get.

Delivery

Edited photos are delivered within 48 hours. High resolution, watermarked with my handle. Don't crop or cover the watermark when you post.

What "edited" means

Color, exposure, contrast, cropping, sharpening. Not changing how someone looks. I don't slim, reshape, or retouch bodies.

Refunds

All sales are final once files are delivered. If a file is corrupted or I sent the wrong person, I'll fix it free. If I don't deliver at all, you get a full refund.

Rights

I keep the copyright to every photo. When you buy, you can post them on personal social media, print them for yourself, and share them with family. Recruiting profiles are fine.

You can't:

  • Sell or license them
  • Hand them to a business or news outlet
  • Crop out my credit
  • Run them through an AI tool

Credit @shotbyjaheim_ when you post.

I may use photos in my portfolio and promo material. Tell me before you order if you'd rather I didn't.

Weather

Game cancelled means your order rolls to the makeup date or the next game, or you get a refund.

Questions
